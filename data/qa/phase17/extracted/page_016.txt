Mizoram Fails to Receive SASCI Funds in
2026-27, Opposition Demands Explanation from
CM Lalduhoma 
The Assam Tribune 2026-10-04T05:49:20
As of September 30, 2026, seven of the eight North Eastern States had received SASCI funds for 2026-27, while
Mizoram received zero rupees. The allocations for other states were as follows: Arunachal Pradesh received Rs 3,965.82
crore, Nagaland Rs 3,324.68 crore, Tripura Rs 2,613.17 crore, Assam Rs 2,504.16 crore, Manipur Rs 2,129.24 crore,
Meghalaya Rs 1,947.14 crore, and Sikkim Rs 921.97 crore. The SASCI scheme provides states with 50-year interest-free
loans for capital investment projects, with the Union Government allocating Rs 2 lakh crore for 2026-27 under this
scheme. 
The MNF highlighted that Mizoram has faced difficulties in availing SASCI funds since the Zoram People’s Movement
(ZPM) Government took power in December 2023. The party alleged that Rs 233.31 crore sanctioned to Mizoram under
SASCI in 2023-24 had lapsed, and another Rs 111.52 crore sanctioned in 2025-26 suffered the same fate. The Opposition
attributed this to poor financial management by Chief Minister Lalduhoma and demanded an explanation from him in his
capacity as both Chief Minister and Finance Minister. 
The MNF further alleged that the Mizoram Government had previously submitted utilisation certificates without releasing
the corresponding SASCI funds. The party stated it would monitor the issue closely and take up any such matter with the
Centre if similar instances occurred in the future. The Department of Expenditure, the Union Government’s nodal
department for state finances, including the release of special assistance, was not directly quoted but referenced in the
context of the RTI reply. 
The Opposition’s press release underscores the urgency of addressing the financial discrepancies, with the MNF insisting
that the zero fund release for 2026-27 warrants a formal explanation from Lalduhoma.
