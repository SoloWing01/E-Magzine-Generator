NEC Pledges Support for Nagaland’s Coffee Farmers, Advocates Modernisation and Integrated Development
The North Eastern Council (NEC) has reaffirmed its commitment to supporting Nagaland’s coffee farmers by addressing
key challenges such as water harvesting, mechanisation, and the adoption of new technologies and equipment. Speaking
during a review of the Coffee USP Project in Tuophema village, Kohima district, NEC Secretary Satinder Kumar Bhalla
emphasized that these measures are essential for the welfare and economic upliftment of coffee-growing communities.
