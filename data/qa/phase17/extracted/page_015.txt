As of **September 30, 2026**, seven of the eight North Eastern States had received SASCI funds, while Mizoram received **zero rupees**. The
allocations for other states were as follows: **Arunachal Pradesh (Rs 3,965.82 crore)**, **Nagaland (Rs 3,324.68 crore)**, **Tripura (Rs 2,613.17
crore)**, **Assam (Rs 2,504.16 crore)**, **Manipur (Rs 2,129.24 crore)**, **Meghalaya (Rs 1,947.14 crore)**, and **Sikkim (Rs 921.97 crore)**.
The SASCI scheme provides **50-year interest-free loans** for capital investment projects, with the Union Government allocating **Rs 2 lakh
crore** for 2026-27.
The MNF highlighted that Mizoram has faced difficulties in availing SASCI funds since the **Zoram People’s Movement (ZPM) Government** took
office in **December 2023**. The party alleged that **Rs 233.31 crore** sanctioned under SASCI in **2023-24** and **Rs 111.52 crore**
sanctioned in **2025-26** had **lapsed** due to poor financial management. The MNF specifically accused Chief Minister Lalduhoma, who also
holds the **Finance portfolio**, of failing to utilize available Central funds effectively.
The Opposition party further alleged that the Mizoram Government had previously submitted **utilization certificates** without releasing the
corresponding SASCI funds. The MNF stated it would **monitor the issue closely** and take up any such matters with the Centre if similar instances
occurred in the future. The **Department of Expenditure**, the nodal department for State finances, was not directly quoted but referenced in the
context of fund releases.
The MNF demanded an explanation from Lalduhoma regarding the **zero fund release** in 2026-27, emphasizing his dual role as both **Chief
Minister and Finance Minister**.
