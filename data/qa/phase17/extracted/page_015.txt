Mizoram Fails to Receive SASCI Funds in
2026-27, Opposition Demands Explanation from
CM Lalduhoma 
The Assam Tribune 2026-10-04T05:49:20
The Mizoram Government has received no funds under the Centre’s Special Assistance to States
for Capital Investment (SASCI) scheme during the current financial year, according to an RTI
reply from the Department of Expenditure, Ministry of Finance. The Opposition Mizo National
Front (MNF) has cited this information in a press release, raising concerns over the state’s
financial management under Chief Minister Pu Lalduhoma, who also holds the Finance portfolio.
