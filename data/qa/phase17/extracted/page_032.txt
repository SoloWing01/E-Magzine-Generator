Guwahati’s 800-Run ODI Sparks Debate: Was the Pitch Truly ‘Good’?
Guwahati recently hosted an ODI cricket match that shattered records and redefined expectations, but the spectacle left
many questioning whether the pitch lived up to the ideals of the format. The match between India and West Indies produced
**811 runs in total**, the third-highest aggregate in ODI history, with **five centuries**—three for West Indies and two for
India—and a **405-run chase** completed in just **43.3 overs** with eight wickets in hand. The West Indies’ total of **405
for seven** was their highest in ODI cricket, setting the stage for one of the most one-sided run-fests ever seen.
