Mizoram Receives No SASCI Funds in 2026-27, Opposition Demands Explanation from CM Lalduhoma
The Mizoram Government has not received any funds under the Centre’s **Special Assistance to States for Capital
Investment (SASCI)** scheme during the current financial year (2026-27), according to an RTI reply from the Department
of Expenditure, Ministry of Finance. The Opposition **Mizo National Front (MNF)** has cited this information in a press
release, raising concerns over the state’s financial management under Chief Minister **Pu Lalduhoma**.
