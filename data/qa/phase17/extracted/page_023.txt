Search Suspended After 71-Year-Old Man Found
Dead in Manipur’s Kangpokpi Forest 
The Assam Tribune 2026-10-04T12:00:08
Haokip, a resident of Khunkho Kuki Village near Leimakhong, had gone missing after his field hut was allegedly set
ablaze on Saturday. His body was later discovered with his hands reportedly tied behind his back, according to sources.
His farm trousers, shirt, and sweater were recovered during a search operation on Sunday, which involved the deployment
of sniffer dogs and intensified efforts by security forces. 
The search for Haokip began after villagers noticed his hut was burnt and he was missing. Heavy rainfall on Saturday had
kept many villagers away from their fields, and the incident came to light only in the evening. Haokip was known to
spend nights in his field hut. Despite a search that continued until dark, he could not be located, and the operation was
suspended due to darkness and security concerns. 
The Kuki Youth Organisation (KYO), Leimakhong Block, had earlier called for Haokip’s immediate and safe return,
urging authorities and security agencies to intensify the search and take action against those responsible. Meanwhile, the
Ariang (Khunkhu) Naga Village Authority issued a clarification, stating that their village and residents had no
involvement in Haokip’s disappearance. The authority emphasized that the two villages had maintained peaceful and
cordial relations for decades and appealed against unverified allegations, warning that such claims could create
unnecessary distrust and tension. 
The village authority also mentioned that movement restrictions had been imposed on its residents from August 14 as a
precautionary measure following an earlier incident involving Naga villages. The incident involving Haokip’s
disappearance and death occurs amid renewed tensions between Kuki and Naga communities in Manipur, particularly in
Kangpokpi and neighboring areas, where reports of abductions, hostage-taking, and attacks involving civilians have been
reported in recent months. 
Security forces continued search and security operations in the area, but further details surrounding Haokip’s death
remain awaited.
