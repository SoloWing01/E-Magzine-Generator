71-Year-Old Kuki Man Found Dead in Manipur’s Kangpokpi Forest After Alleged Abduction
A 71-year-old man, identified as Hemjang Haokip of Khunkho Kuki Village near Leimakhong in Manipur’s Kangpokpi
district, was found dead in a nearby forest on Sunday following his reported abduction and the burning of his field hut.
Haokip had gone missing after his hut was allegedly set ablaze on Saturday, with his body later discovered with his hands
tied behind his back. His farm trousers, shirt, and sweater were recovered during a search operation conducted by villagers
and security forces, who deployed sniffer dogs to locate him [Source 31].
