Final Campaign Day in Nagaon Bypoll: BJP,
Congress, AIUDF Trade Barbs as Polls Near 
The Assam Tribune 2026-10-04T12:21:04
BJP leaders, including Assam Minister and party leader Pijush Hazarika, targeted AIUDF chief Maulana Badruddin Ajmal
and Congress MLA Asif Mohammad Nazar during a rally in Lahorighat. Hazarika criticized Ajmal’s health, describing
him as "an ailing man" who "would not be able to work without assistance." He urged Ajmal to stop campaigning and
seek treatment in Dubai, stating, "He has to be supported and helped by two people." The BJP’s campaign also saw
participation from state president Dilip Saikia and Finance Minister Jayanta Mallabaruah, who addressed supporters in
Raha. Saikia emphasized voter confidence in the BJP, declaring, "People support BJP because they know the things that
the party has been doing for them." He further urged voters to support Devasish Sharma for the bypolls, as supporters
chanted in unison. 
The Congress held campaign meetings in Lahorighat and Raha, rallying behind candidate Sibamoni Bora. APCC
president Gaurav Gogoi, AICC secretary Manoj Chauhan, and MLAs Asif Mohammad Nazar and Baby Begum attended
the gatherings, which the party described as reflecting its focus on "unity, harmony, democracy, and development." Gogoi
directed sharp criticism at AIUDF and Ajmal, alleging a close political alliance between Ajmal and Assam Chief Minister
Himanta Biswa Sarma. He claimed Ajmal had functioned as the BJP’s "B Team" and predicted that the AIUDF would
cease to exist after the bypolls, with Sarma eventually inducting Ajmal into the BJP. Gogoi stated, "Maulana Badruddin
Ajmal is the closest friend of Himanta Biswa Sarma in Assam politics." 
The Nagaon bypoll was triggered by the resignation of former Congress MP Pradyut Bordoloi from the Lok Sabha seat,
who later won the Dispur Assembly seat on a BJP ticket. With campaigning now over, the constituency observes the
silence period until polling day on October 6, with counting scheduled for October 9.
