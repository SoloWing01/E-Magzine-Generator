NEC Pledges Comprehensive Support to

The Assam Tribune 2026-10-04T05:36:04
The North Eastern Council (NEC) has reaffirmed its commitment to bolstering Nagaland’s coffee
farming community, with Secretary Satinder Kumar Bhalla emphasizing the need for
modernization and integrated development during a recent review of the Coffee USP Project in
Tuophema village, Kohima district.
