NFR Rolls Out Major Infrastructure Upgrades

To address rising demand and operational challenges, the NFR has launched multiple infrastructure projects across Guwahati and its surrounding areas.
One key initiative is the **Kamakhya-Guwahati third line**, a 5.74-kilometer stretch sanctioned at a cost of Rs 467 crore. Scheduled for completion
by 2028, this project aims to ease congestion, enhance capacity, and improve train operations for passengers in Kamrup Metro district [Source 15].
Another major undertaking is the **Agthori-Kamakhya double line**, combined with the construction of a **second Saraighat Rail-cum-Road
Bridge**. The Rs 1,474 crore project spans 7.20 kilometers, including a 1.3-kilometer bridge that will carry a double-line railway track alongside a
three-lane road. This infrastructure is expected to relieve pressure on the existing Saraighat Bridge, boost connectivity between Guwahati, North
Guwahati, and other Northeast regions, and support additional commuter services [Source 15].
In parallel, the **Mega Coaching Complex at New Guwahati** is under construction at a cost of Rs 227 crore. This facility will strengthen terminal
and maintenance infrastructure, further supporting the region’s expanding rail network [Source 15].
Redevelopment efforts are also transforming Guwahati and Kamakhya railway stations. Guwahati Railway Station is being modernized with an IT park
and designed under green-building principles, incorporating solar power, waste management, and water conservation measures [Source 15].
Meanwhile, the **Kamakhya-Azara Elevated Corridor** project includes a 13.25-kilometer double-line stretch, with the remaining portion targeted for
completion by July 2028. This elevated viaduct, particularly through the Deepor Beel area, aims to balance railway efficiency with wildlife
conservation, ensuring safe passage for elephants and other species [Source 15].
The NFR is also enhancing freight and logistics capabilities through **Gati Shakti Cargo Terminals (GCTs)**. The GCT at Baihata, near Guwahati,
has already been completed, improving freight-handling and logistics operations in the region [Source 15].
These projects collectively seek to expand railway capacity, reduce congestion, and improve the resilience of train operations in and around Guwahati
[Source 15].
