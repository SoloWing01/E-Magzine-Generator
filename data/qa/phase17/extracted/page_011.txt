Guwahati Railway Station Expands Capacity with
Major Infrastructure Projects 
The Assam Tribune 2026-10-04T07:25:14
One of the key initiatives is the construction of the Kamakhya-Guwahati third line, a 5.74-kilometer project sanctioned at
a cost of Rs 467 crore. Scheduled for completion by 2028, this additional line aims to increase capacity, reduce
congestion, and improve train operations for passengers and residents of Kamrup Metro district. 
Another significant project is the Agthori-Kamakhya double line, which includes the second Saraighat Rail-cum-Road
Bridge. The Rs 1,474 crore project spans 7.20 kilometers, featuring a 1.3-kilometer bridge that will carry a double-line
railway track alongside a three-lane road. This infrastructure is expected to enhance rail and road connectivity across the
Brahmaputra while easing pressure on the existing Saraighat Bridge. 
The development of a Mega Coaching Complex at New Guwahati is also underway, with a sanctioned cost of Rs 227
crore. This facility will bolster train terminal and maintenance capabilities in the region. Additionally, the redevelopment
of Guwahati and Kamakhya railway stations is in progress, focusing on modern amenities and improved accessibility.
Guwahati Railway Station is being transformed into a green-building facility, incorporating solar power, waste
management, and water management systems. 
To address environmental and wildlife concerns, the Kamakhya-Azara Elevated Corridor is being constructed. This
project includes an elevated viaduct along the stretch, including sections near Deepor Beel, to ensure safe wildlife
movement while maintaining efficient rail connectivity. The remaining 13.25-kilometer Azara-Kamakhya double-line
stretch is targeted for completion by July 2028. 
The NFR is also enhancing freight and logistics infrastructure through Gati Shakti Cargo Terminals. The GCT at Baihata,
located near Guwahati, has already been completed, improving freight-handling and logistics operations in the region.
These projects collectively aim to expand railway capacity, reduce congestion, and enhance the efficiency of train
services around Guwahati.
