Roaming Cattle and Roadside Garbage Spark Civic Concerns in Lumding
In Lumding, the issue of freely roaming cattle on public roads has become a growing concern, raising questions about road
safety and civic responsibility. Residents have reported seeing cows and bulls occupying roadside areas and even standing on
carriageways, posing risks to both motorists and pedestrians. The presence of scattered garbage along the roadsides further
compounds the problem, highlighting sanitation and civic management challenges.
