Final Campaign Day in Nagaon Bypoll: BJP,
Congress, AIUDF Trade Barbs as Polls Near 
The Assam Tribune 2026-10-04T12:21:04
The campaign for the Nagaon Lok Sabha bypoll concluded at 5 pm on Sunday, October 4, with
the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP), Indian National Congress (Congress), and All India United
Democratic Front (AIUDF) making their last appeals to voters ahead of polling on October 6.
Senior leaders from all three parties intensified their efforts as the constituency entered the
mandatory 48-hour silence period, which began immediately after the campaign ended.
