BJP leaders, including Assam Minister and party leader Pijush Hazarika, targeted AIUDF chief Maulana Badruddin Ajmal and Congress MLA Asif
Mohammad Nazar during a campaign meeting in Lahorighat. Hazarika criticized Ajmal’s health, describing him as "an ailing man" who "would not be
able to work without assistance." He urged Ajmal to stop campaigning and seek treatment in Dubai, adding that such a person would need support from
two others to function. The BJP’s state president, Dilip Saikia, and Finance Minister Jayanta Mallabaruah also participated in campaign activities in the
Raha Assembly segment, with Saikia emphasizing voter support for BJP’s candidate Devasish Sharma. Saikia claimed that the party’s efforts had
resonated across the constituency, with supporters chanting for Sharma’s victory.
The Congress held campaign meetings in Lahorighat and Raha, rallying behind Sibamoni Bora. APCC president Gaurav Gogoi, AICC secretary Manoj
Chauhan, and MLAs Asif Mohammad Nazar and Baby Begum addressed supporters, with the party highlighting themes of "unity, harmony,
democracy, and development." Gogoi directed sharp criticism at AIUDF and Ajmal, alleging a close political relationship between Ajmal and Assam
Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma. He claimed Ajmal had functioned as the BJP’s "B Team" and predicted that the AIUDF would cease to exist
after the bypoll, with Sarma eventually inducting Ajmal into the BJP.
The Nagaon bypoll was triggered by the resignation of former Congress MP Pradyut Bordoloi from the Lok Sabha seat, who later won the Dispur
Assembly seat on a BJP ticket. With campaigning now over, the constituency entered the silence period at 5 pm on October 4, with voting scheduled
for October 6 and counting on October 9.
