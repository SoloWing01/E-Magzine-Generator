Final Campaign Day in Nagaon Bypoll: BJP, Congress, AIUDF Sharpen Attacks as Polls Near
The campaign for the Nagaon Lok Sabha bypoll concluded at 5 pm on Sunday, October 4, with the Bharatiya Janata Party
(BJP), Indian National Congress (Congress), and All India United Democratic Front (AIUDF) making their last appeals to
voters ahead of polling on October 6. The final day saw senior leaders from all three parties intensify their efforts and trade
barbs as the constituency entered the mandatory 48-hour silence period.
