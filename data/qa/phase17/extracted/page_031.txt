AASU Raises Concerns Over CJP’s School Visits,
Cites Alleged Political Motives 
The Assam Tribune 2026-10-04T08:18:13
AASU general secretary Samiran Phukan made the remarks following CJP’s launch of its campaign, during which its
functionaries visited government schools in Palashbari and Dispur constituencies to assess infrastructure and facilities.
Phukan stated that while developmental activities are welcome, they should not be driven by political motives. "It would
have been a different thing if they concentrated on the development of Assam, but it’s all politics," he said. 
The AASU leader emphasized that the organization’s actions are understood by the public to be politically driven.
"People understand that their actions are political. We, the All Assam Students’ Union, have worked for the people of
Assam and will keep on doing that," Phukan added. 
CJP’s campaign began after a regional volunteers’ meeting in Guwahati on September 27, where around 35 members,
including co-convener Ashutosh Ranka, were detained by Assam Police for allegedly holding the gathering without prior
permission. The detained members were later released after initiating a hunger strike. 
The AASU has urged the public to identify and recognize the forces it believes are attempting to create instability in the
state. The organization has also stated its commitment to continuing its work for the people of Assam.
