Nagaon Bypoll: Alleged Pushback of Women
Sparks Political Debate 
The Assam Tribune 2026-10-04T09:38:43
Assam Congress president Gaurav Gogoi has written to Union External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar, seeking urgent
action to trace and bring back Jahanara Begum of Bagariguri and Mumtaz Begum of Dhing Gaon. According to Gogoi’s
letter dated October 2, the two women were allegedly sent across the India-Bangladesh border through Gate No. 2 near
Border Pillar No. 1339/MP during the night of June 13–14, 2026. Their whereabouts remain unknown since then. 
The case stems from Foreigners Tribunal proceedings in Nagaon, where both women were declared foreigners in 2019.
They challenged the tribunal’s decisions in the Gauhati High Court, which in April 2026 set aside the earlier opinions and
remanded the cases for fresh consideration due to insufficient material evidence, as per Gogoi’s letter. 
Following the High Court’s directions, Jahanara appeared before the tribunal on May 29, and Mumtaz on May 30. Gogoi
alleged that both women were taken into custody shortly afterward, preventing them from obtaining fresh tribunal
opinions or challenging them in court. 
The Gauhati High Court later observed that the tribunal office had ‘deliberately and wilfully delayed’ issuing a certified
copy of its opinion in Mumtaz’s case, ruling that the state machinery had obstructed her right to approach the High Court.
Similar findings were recorded in Jahanara’s case, with the court also describing the June 2 expulsion orders—directing
the women to leave India within 24 hours—as ‘farcical’. 
The High Court directed the Ministry of External Affairs to locate the women in Bangladesh and facilitate their return to
India for legal recourse. Meanwhile, the case has become entangled in the bypoll campaign, with Mumtaz’s son appealing
to political parties to prioritize finding her rather than politicizing the issue. 
He urged both the Congress and AIUDF to assist in tracing her, stating, ‘We want to find her, where is she,’ and expressed
a desire to meet Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma and BJP’s Nagaon candidate Devashish Sharma, who was the
District Commissioner when the expulsion order was issued.
