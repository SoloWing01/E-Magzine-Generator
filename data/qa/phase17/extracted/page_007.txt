Assam Rifles Seize Rs 5.07 Crore Heroin in Mizoram, Two Myanmar Nationals Arrested
In a significant operation targeting drug trafficking along the India-Myanmar border, the Assam Rifles seized **2.5 kg of
heroin** valued at approximately **Rs 5.07 crore** in Mizoram’s Champhai district. The operation resulted in the
apprehension of **two Myanmar nationals**.
