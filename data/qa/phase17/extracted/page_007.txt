Assam Rifles Seize Rs 5.07 Crore Heroin in
Mizoram, Apprehend Two Myanmar Nationals 
The Assam Tribune 2026-10-04T06:59:55
In a significant operation targeting drug trafficking along the India-Myanmar border, the Assam
Rifles seized 2.5 kg of heroin valued at approximately Rs 5.07 crore and apprehended two
Myanmar nationals in Mizoram’s Champhai district. The operation was conducted at Zote,
Champhai, based on specific intelligence gathered by the troops. Following the seizure, the
narcotics and the arrested individuals were handed over to the Champhai Excise and Narcotics
Department for further investigation and legal proceedings, as reported by a Defence spokesman.
