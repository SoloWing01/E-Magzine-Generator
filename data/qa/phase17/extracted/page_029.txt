AASU Accuses CJP of Political Motives Behind School Visits in Assam
The All Assam Students’ Union (AASU) has raised concerns over the Cockroach Janata Party’s (CJP) recent school visits in
Assam, alleging that the initiative is driven by a ‘political agenda’ aimed at maintaining instability in the state. AASU
general secretary Samiran Phukan made the remarks following the launch of CJP’s ‘School Thik Karo’ campaign, which
involves assessing infrastructure and facilities in government schools across Assam.
