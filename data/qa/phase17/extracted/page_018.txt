NEC Pledges Comprehensive Support to
Nagaland’s Coffee Sector Amid Push for
Modernization 
The Assam Tribune 2026-10-04T05:36:04
During his visit, Bhalla inspected coffee plantation sites and the Hi-Tech Coffee Washing Station, engaging directly with
local growers and cluster members. He underscored that the Coffee USP Project extends beyond mere plantation
initiatives, framing it as an ‘integrated livelihood and economic development programme’ designed to maximize value
from the region’s natural and human resources. 
Bhalla highlighted critical gaps hindering the sector’s competitiveness, stating that commercial viability requires ‘good
road connectivity, modern processing machinery, efficient post-harvest infrastructure, quality assurance, value addition,
and strong market and export linkages.’ He stressed that these improvements are essential for transforming coffee
cultivation into a sustainable economic driver for rural communities. 
The NEC Secretary also urged the Nagaland Government and the Coffee Board to collaborate with Central agricultural
institutions to identify suitable intercrops and complementary activities for coffee plantations. Such diversification, he
noted, would enhance land productivity and provide farmers with additional income streams, thereby strengthening the
sector’s economic resilience. 
Bhalla’s remarks followed a broader directive from North Eastern States’ Chief Ministers to devise strategies aimed at
significantly boosting farmers’ incomes. He reiterated that achieving this goal demands coordinated efforts from the
Government of India, Nagaland Government, Coffee Board, local administration, farmers, technical institutions, and the
private sector. 
The visit concluded with Bhalla expressing optimism that the project would lead to a ‘substantial increase in the income
of farmers and communities,’ contingent upon addressing challenges like water harvesting, mechanization, and
technology adoption.
