Assam Congress president Gaurav Gogoi has written to Union External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar, seeking urgent action to trace and bring back
Jahanara Begum of Bagariguri and Mumtaz Begum of Dhing Gaon. According to Gogoi’s letter dated October 2, the two women were allegedly sent
across the India-Bangladesh border through Gate No. 2 near Border Pillar No. 1339/MP during the night of June 13–14, 2026. Their whereabouts
remain unknown since then [Source 12].
The case stems from proceedings before the Foreigners Tribunal in Nagaon, where both women were declared foreigners in 2019. They challenged the
tribunal’s opinions before the Gauhati High Court, which in April 2026 set aside the earlier rulings and remanded the cases for fresh consideration due
to insufficient material evidence, as per Gogoi’s letter [Source 12].
Despite the High Court’s directions, Jahanara appeared before the tribunal on May 29 and Mumtaz on May 30. Gogoi alleged that both women were
taken into custody shortly afterward, preventing them from obtaining fresh tribunal opinions or challenging them in court [Source 12].
The Gauhati High Court later examined the June 2 expulsion orders issued by the District Commissioner, Nagaon, directing the women to leave India
within 24 hours—even though they were already in state custody. The court described Mumtaz’s expulsion order as "farcical" and ruled that the state
machinery had deliberately delayed issuing a certified copy of the tribunal’s opinion, effectively blocking her legal recourse [Source 12].
The High Court also directed the Ministry of External Affairs to be impleaded in the matter to locate the women in Bangladesh and facilitate their
return for further legal proceedings [Source 12].
The issue has now entered the bypoll campaign, with Mumtaz’s son appealing to political parties to avoid politicizing her case. He urged the Congress
and AIUDF to focus on finding her rather than using her situation for electoral gains. "We want to find her, where is she," he told reporters, adding that
he hoped to meet Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma and BJP’s Nagaon candidate Devashish Sharma, who was the District Commissioner when the
expulsion order was issued [Source 12].
