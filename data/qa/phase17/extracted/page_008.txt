The recovery took place in **Zote, Champhai district**, with the seized narcotics and the arrested individuals subsequently handed over to the
**Champhai Excise and Narcotics Department** for further investigation and legal proceedings.
This latest seizure underscores the Assam Rifles’ ongoing efforts, in coordination with civil agencies, to combat drug-smuggling activities along the
international border and safeguard the security of border populations.
The operation follows a recent **destruction of seized drugs** worth over **Rs 11.82 crore** in Mizoram. The disposal, conducted at **Bethani
Multipurpose Farm in Aizawl**, included **5.356 kg of heroin**, **19.430 kg of ganja**, **1.985 kg of methamphetamine tablets**, and other
narcotic substances. The destruction adhered to **Section 51A of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, 1985**, and the
**NDPS (Seizure, Storage, Sampling and Disposal) Rules, 2022**.
Mizoram and neighboring **Manipur** remain critical regions for drug trafficking due to their **910 km of unfenced international border** with
Myanmar. The porous border facilitates the smuggling of heroin, methamphetamine (commonly referred to as **Yaba or party tablets**), codeine-
based cough syrups, and other narcotics from Myanmar into India and beyond.
