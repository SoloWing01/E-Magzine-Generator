AASU Raises Concerns Over CJP’s School Visits,
Cites Alleged Political Motives 
The Assam Tribune 2026-10-04T08:18:13
The All Assam Students’ Union (AASU) has expressed reservations about the recent activities of
the Cockroach Janata Party (CJP) in Assam, specifically its school visits under the "School Thik
Karo" campaign. The AASU alleges that these initiatives may carry a "political agenda aimed at
keeping the situation volatile."
