NORTHEAST SENTINEL AI
Grounded AI Editorial Generation
The editorial generator does not treat the language model as an independent news source.
Selected articles are embedded with Sentence Transformers and stored in Chroma. The requested story is used to retrieve
relevant evidence, which is passed to Mistral as the generation context.
Generated factual paragraphs carry source citations, and the grounding stage compares claims with their cited evidence
before an article is accepted.
