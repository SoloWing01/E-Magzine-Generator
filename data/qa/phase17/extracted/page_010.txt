Guwahati Railway Station Surges Past 50,000 Daily Passengers as NFR Rolls Out Major Infrastructure Upgrades
Guwahati Railway Station is currently managing over 50,000 passengers daily, with numbers peaking at around 65,000
during high-traffic seasons, according to the Northeast Frontier Railway (NFR). The station serves as a critical transit hub
for the Northeast, with more than 200 trains operating through it every day, reflecting the region’s growing reliance on rail
connectivity [Source 15].
