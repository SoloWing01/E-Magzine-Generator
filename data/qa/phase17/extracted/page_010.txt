Guwahati Railway Station Expands Capacity with
Major Infrastructure Projects 
The Assam Tribune 2026-10-04T07:25:14
Guwahati Railway Station is currently managing over 50,000 passengers daily, with numbers
rising to approximately 65,000 during peak seasons. This surge in passenger traffic has prompted
the Northeast Frontier Railway (NFR) to accelerate infrastructure development projects across
Guwahati and its surrounding areas.
