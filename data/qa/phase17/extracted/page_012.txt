Assam CM Highlights Land Shortage as Key Industrial Hurdle; Govt Pushes for Land Banks and Estates
Assam Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma has identified the shortage of suitable land as a major barrier to industrial
growth in the state, stating that many potential investors are unable to establish operations due to this constraint. Speaking
at the inauguration of PepsiCo India’s Rs 787-crore food manufacturing plant in Banekuchi, Nalbari, Sarma emphasized the
government’s plan to address this issue by creating land banks and developing industrial estates at strategic locations.
