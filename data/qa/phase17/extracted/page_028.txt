Roaming cattle and roadside garbage pose civic
challenges in Lumding 
The Assam Tribune 2026-10-04T06:06:13
The issue of freely roaming cattle on public roads in Lumding has become a growing concern,
raising questions about road safety and civic management. Residents have observed cows and
bulls occupying roadside areas and even standing on carriageways, creating potential hazards for
commuters. Garbage scattered along the roadside further compounds the problem, highlighting
sanitation concerns and the need for better civic oversight.
