METHODOLOGY 
Reading the Edition 
01 Web collection 
Articles are discovered and collected through the scraping pipeline.
02 Validation and date filtering 
Article metadata and publication dates are validated against the configured coverage period.
03 Relevance and classification 
Articles are evaluated for Northeast relevance and classified into magazine themes.
04 Scoring and ranking 
Relevance, theme, security, freshness, source quality and content quality contribute to article ranking.
05 Retrieval and grounded generation 
Selected articles are embedded into the vector store and retrieved as evidence for grounded editorial generation.
