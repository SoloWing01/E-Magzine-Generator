"We need land. Many industries want to come to Assam, but due to the absence of suitable land, they could not come. So, now we are trying to create
land banks. Wherever there is land, we will take industries there," Sarma said, underlining the urgency of the situation. The government is prioritizing
land near railway stations and highways for industrial estate development to enhance connectivity and accessibility for businesses.
The newly inaugurated PepsiCo facility, which will manufacture food products, is expected to generate over 200 direct and 500 indirect employment
opportunities, with approximately 75% of the workforce comprising women. Sarma noted that around 120 women will receive digital literacy training
as part of the initiative. The project also marks a significant milestone, as it will enable Assam to produce food products that will be distributed
nationwide, previously manufactured primarily in states like Uttar Pradesh, Karnataka, and Maharashtra.
The facility’s operations will create a substantial demand for local potatoes, with PepsiCo currently requiring 30,000 metric tonnes annually, a figure
projected to rise to 70,000 metric tonnes by 2030. To support local farmers, the state government, PepsiCo, and the State Bank of India have
collaborated to facilitate the procurement of specialized potato seeds through Kisan Credit Cards, benefiting around 5,000 farmers and their families.
Additionally, a Rs 35-crore cold storage facility with a capacity of 10,000 tonnes is being established to strengthen the potato supply chain.
Sarma also highlighted the importance of retaining Assam’s workforce within the state to reduce reliance on external employment opportunities. He
referenced the government’s Shraddhanjali scheme, which has, in some months, repatriated as many as 50 bodies of workers who died while working
in other states, including Karnataka, Telangana, Maharashtra, and Kerala. "To prevent such situations, we need to establish industries and create
employment opportunities within Assam so that our people do not have to leave the state in search of work," he stated.
The Chief Minister further revealed that Assam has attracted investments totaling around Rs 3 lakh crore across sectors such as hydrocarbons and
petrochemicals, energy, mining, and electronics, with the potential to generate employment for approximately one lakh people.
